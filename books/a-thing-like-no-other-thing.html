A Thing Like No Other Thing

Imagination, curiosity, and the questions of a younger writer.

Sometimes the most interesting questions begin with the seemingly ordinary.

A Thing Like No Other Thing brings together early writings exploring the curious, humorous, philosophical, and occasionally absurd experience of being human.

Written in 1987, these pieces offer a glimpse into the imagination of a young writer discovering writing as a way of thinking, questioning, and exploring the world.

About the Book Contents Selected Readings

About the Book

These writings belong to an earlier period of my life, when writing was becoming an increasingly important means of exploring ideas and experiences.

Through stories, reflections, humor, and imagination, the collection considers the familiar from unfamiliar perspectives.

Looking back at these writings decades later offers an opportunity to encounter the person who originally wrote them.

Ideas and Themes

  • Imagination and curiosity
  • Philosophical inquiry
  • Humor and absurdity
  • Language and perception
  • Writing as discovery

A Note from the Author

There is something curious about reading one's own writing nearly forty years later.

The person who wrote these pieces did not know the life that lay ahead of him. Yet many of the questions he was asking remain familiar to me today.
