Personal Reflection

Think Like a Child

By Bradley Arnold

I think like a child.

Everything I see carries a little absurdity with it. I laugh more easily than I should. I ask questions that don’t always have answers. I find myself wondering about things most people seem willing to ignore.

Perhaps that’s because this whole thing we call life feels strangely temporary.

Everything eventually leaves.

People.

Moments.

Ideas.

Entire civilizations.

Even galaxies.

And someday…even atoms, protons, and stuff even smaller.

Everything gone.

Knowing that doesn’t sadden me. It makes me notice.

So I laugh at everything.

I cackle at nonsense.

I giggle at my cat.

I chortle with strangers.

And I choose to show my best.

Why?

I don’t really know.

Just because, I guess.

Thinking like a child.

The refusal to stop wondering.

Refusing to stop noticing.

Refusing to stop laughing.

While still growing up.
