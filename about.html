About Bradley Arnold

Writer. Educator. Traveler. Artist. Lifelong learner.

I have spent much of my life asking questions, exploring unfamiliar ideas, encountering different ways of living, and trying to understand something of the experience of being human.

Along the way, I have been a teacher, a student, a traveler, a writer, and many other things.

Each of these experiences has offered a different perspective, though none has provided a complete understanding of who I am or what life is about.

This website brings together some of those experiences and the writings, ideas, and creative works that have emerged from them.

The Writer

Writing as a way of thinking.

For nearly forty years, writing has been one of my principal ways of exploring ideas, experiences, questions, and uncertainties.

My writings have taken many forms: short stories, philosophical essays, poetry, journals, memoirs, and personal reflections.

Some of my earlier writings have remained with me for decades, gradually becoming part of a conversation between the person who originally wrote them and the person revisiting them today.

I have often discovered that writing is not simply a means of expressing what I already understand. It is also a means of discovering what I do not yet understand.

The Educator

Teaching, learning, and thinking.

Education has been an important part of my life, both as a teacher and as a learner.

My teaching experiences have taken me across different countries, cultures, educational systems, and learning environments.

My interests include language education, critical thinking, writing, intercultural understanding, and the development of independent learners.

I currently work in education in Columbus, Ohio, teaching English and supporting the development of English language learners and their teachers.

The Ten Stairs of Critical Thinking

One framework I have developed for thinking about learning is the Ten Stairs of Critical Thinking.

The framework encourages learners to move beyond recognition and memorization toward increasingly meaningful forms of understanding, communication, and reflection.

1. Identify

Recognizing.

2. Define

Establishing meaning.

3. Illustrate

Making understanding visible.

4. Describe

Exploring characteristics.

5. Narrate

Communicating experiences.

6. Explain

Exploring how and why.

7. Analyze

Examining relationships.

8. Evaluate

Considering significance.

9. Teach

Sharing understanding.

10. Reflect

Continuing to learn.

These Stairs have applications beyond the classroom. They offer a way of approaching experiences, ideas, relationships, and our continuing development as human beings.

The Artist

Discovering other ways of seeing.

Art offers opportunities to explore experiences and ideas that are not always easily expressed through words.

My creative interests include painting, photography, illustration, and experiments with color, shape, perspective, and composition.

Some of my paintings revisit experiences from decades ago. Others emerge from the ordinary pleasures of everyday life.

The process of creating has become as interesting to me as the finished work itself.

Recovery & Living

Learning to participate in life.

My journey into sobriety began in November 2021.

Recovery has gradually become an exploration of much more than sobriety.

It has invited me to reconsider familiar patterns of thinking and behaving, to examine my relationships with myself and others, and to discover different ways of experiencing life.

My reflections on recovery draw upon personal experience, fellowship, secular spirituality, and an ongoing willingness to learn.

Life Beyond Accomplishments

There is more to life than the books we write, the places we visit, the work we do, or the things we accomplish.

I also enjoy music, dancing, painting, gardening, cooking, spending time with my cats, and discovering small pleasures in everyday experiences.

These experiences do not necessarily need to become achievements or lead to something else.

Sometimes the experience itself is enough.

The Continuing Journey

Looking back across different periods of my life, I recognize both continuities and changes in the questions I have asked.

Some questions have remained with me for decades. Others have gradually changed as my experiences and understanding have changed.

I do not regard this website as a completed account of my life. It is an opportunity to bring together different aspects of that life and to continue exploring the relationships between them.
