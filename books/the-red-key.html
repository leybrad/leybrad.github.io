The Red Key

Memory, identity, and the journey toward self-understanding.

How much of who we are today began in moments we barely remember?

The Red Key is a personal journey through childhood, relationships, memory, identity, and recovery. It explores the ways in which our earliest experiences can quietly shape the lives we subsequently lead.

It is a story of looking backward in order to understand the present, and of discovering that understanding the past does not require remaining imprisoned by it.

About the Book

Some memories remain with us throughout our lives without our fully understanding why they have remained.

The Red Key revisits experiences from different periods of my life, examining the connections between childhood, relationships, personal identity, and the patterns of behavior that followed me into adulthood.

Recovery provided an opportunity to reconsider many of those experiences and to question some of the assumptions I had carried about myself for decades.

This is not simply a story about the past. It is also a story about learning to live differently in the present.

A Note from the Author

There is a difference between remembering an experience and understanding it.

Many of the experiences described in this book were familiar to me long before I began writing about them. What was less familiar was the role they had played in shaping my understanding of myself and my relationships with others.

Writing became an opportunity to return to those experiences with different questions, and occasionally to discover something I had not previously recognized.
