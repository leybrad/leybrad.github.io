Through the Prism — Volume II

Encounter

Meeting the unfamiliar, reconsidering the familiar.

Every encounter offers the possibility of seeing the world a little differently.

Encounter explores the experiences, relationships, conversations, and unexpected discoveries that challenge our familiar ways of understanding life.

Through stories and reflections, this volume considers what happens when our ideas meet the experiences of others, and when the world refuses to behave quite as we expect.

About the Volume Contents Selected Readings

About Encounter

We encounter more than people. We encounter ideas, uncertainties, unfamiliar circumstances, and sometimes unfamiliar aspects of ourselves.

The writings in this volume explore the space between what we expect and what we experience, and the possibilities for discovery that emerge from that space.

Ideas and Themes

  • Human encounters and relationships
  • Different ways of seeing
  • Expectations and uncertainty
  • Communication and understanding
  • Discovery through experience

A Note from the Author

Throughout my life, encounters with people, places, and unfamiliar ways of thinking have often left me with more questions than answers. Many of those questions found their way into my writing.
