Welcome

I am a writer, teacher, traveler, artist, and a person in recovery.

Here you will find my books, stories, essays, paintings, and photographs. Some were created nearly forty years ago. Others are quite recent. What connects them is a curiosity about life and what it means to live it.

Every book has to end somewhere.
Conversations, fortunately, are under no such obligation.

About

My life has taken me across continents, cultures, classrooms, and many different ways of understanding the world.

Through it all, I have continued to write, question, learn, and occasionally discover that I understand considerably less than I once thought I did.

This website is a home for some of those discoveries.
