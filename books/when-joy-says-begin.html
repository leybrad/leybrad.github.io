When Joy Says Begin

Recovery, discovery, and learning to live.

Recovery is not simply about learning how not to drink. It is about discovering how to live.

When Joy Says Begin explores recovery, spiritual growth, self-discovery, and the rediscovery of joy through personal reflections on the Twelve Steps.

It is a conversation about honesty, willingness, acceptance, connection, and the possibilities that emerge when we begin to participate more fully in our own lives.

About the Book

Recovery begins with sobriety, but the journey does not end there.

Through reflections on the Twelve Steps, When Joy Says Begin explores the difference between surviving and living, between knowing something intellectually and experiencing it personally.

These writings approach recovery as an ongoing process of discovery rather than a destination. They explore spirituality without requiring religious belief and consider what it means to develop a more honest relationship with ourselves, with others, and with life itself.

A Note from the Author

I did not begin recovery knowing how to live. Much of what I thought I understood about myself, other people, and the world would gradually need to be reconsidered.

Writing became one of the ways I explored those discoveries. Sometimes it helped me understand what I was experiencing. Sometimes it helped me recognize what I had been unwilling to see.

This book is not the conclusion of that journey. It is part of it.
