Stories & Essays

Writing as a way of wondering, discovering, and understanding.

For much of my life, writing has been a way of exploring the world and my relationship with it.

Sometimes that exploration has taken the form of a story. Sometimes an essay, a poem, a journal entry, or a conversation between imaginary characters.

The writings gathered here span different periods of my life, different countries, different experiences, and different ways of understanding what it means to be human.

Some began nearly forty years ago. Others are still being written.

From the Notebooks

Rediscovering earlier conversations.

Behind many of my published writings are years of notebooks, journals, handwritten manuscripts, and unfinished ideas.

Some contain the beginnings of stories that would not be completed until decades later. Others contain questions, observations, and reflections that remain interesting to me today.

This section will explore some of those original writings and the experiences that surrounded them.

Writing Across Time

One of the unexpected pleasures of revisiting my earlier writings has been the opportunity to encounter the person who originally wrote them.

The younger writer did not know the experiences that lay ahead of him. The older writer sometimes discovers that the younger one was asking questions he is still asking today.

This section will explore the relationship between those voices, the development of ideas, and the continuing conversation between past and present.

Thinking, Writing & Learning

Writing and thinking have always been closely connected for me.

As a teacher, I have also been interested in the processes through which we develop understanding, move beyond assumptions, and learn to think independently.

The Ten Stairs of Critical Thinking

1. Identify

Recognize what is there.

2. Define

Establish what it means.

3. Illustrate

Make understanding visible.

4. Describe

Explore characteristics and details.

5. Narrate

Explore events and experiences.

6. Explain

Explore how and why.

7. Analyze

Examine relationships and patterns.

8. Evaluate

Consider evidence and significance.

9. Teach

Share and communicate understanding.

10. Reflect

Reconsider and continue learning.
