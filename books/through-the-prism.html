Through the Prism

An ongoing conversation across nearly four decades.

A three-volume collection of stories, essays, poems, and philosophical reflections exploring the curious experience of being human.

These writings bring together the questions of a younger writer and the reflections of an older one. Some questions have changed. Others are still being asked.

Through imagination, humor, encounters, uncertainty, and discovery, the collection explores the many ways we experience and interpret the world.

About the Collection

Through the Prism brings together writings from different periods of my life. Some began nearly forty years ago, while others emerged much more recently.

Although the settings, characters, and forms of writing vary considerably, many of the pieces share a curiosity about perception, identity, relationships, language, and the nature of understanding.

Rather than presenting a single philosophy or a collection of answers, these volumes invite readers to explore different ways of looking at familiar and unfamiliar experiences.

A Conversation Across Time

When I first wrote many of these pieces, I rarely knew exactly what I was writing about. I usually sensed that something interesting was nearby, just beyond the edge of whatever I understood at the time.

Writing became my way of following it, one sentence at a time.

Looking back, I realize I wasn't simply collecting stories or ideas.

I was collecting questions.

Some of them took forty years to finish asking.

Every book has to end somewhere.
Conversations, fortunately, are under no such obligation.
