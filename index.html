Welcome

For much of my life, I have been collecting questions.

Some have found their way into stories, essays, poems, and books. Others have followed me into classrooms, across continents, through relationships, and into my experiences of recovery. More recently, some have found their way onto canvases.

I have discovered that writing, teaching, traveling, painting, and simply living are not necessarily separate pursuits. They are different ways of encountering the world and discovering something about ourselves.

This website brings together some of those explorations. Some began nearly forty years ago. Others began yesterday.

I invite you to wander through these pages, follow whatever interests you, and perhaps discover a few questions of your own.
