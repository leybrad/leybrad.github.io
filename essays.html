Stories & Essays

The pleasure of wondering where an idea might lead.

For much of my life, writing has been one of my favorite ways of exploring the world and my relationship with it.

Sometimes I begin with a question. Sometimes with a character, an observation, a conversation, or an idea that seems sufficiently ridiculous to be worth exploring.

I rarely know exactly where a piece of writing will take me when I begin. That uncertainty is often part of the pleasure of writing it.

The writings gathered here span nearly four decades, different countries, imagined worlds, personal experiences, and changing ways of understanding life.

Some are serious. Some are playful. Some are both. And some began with questions that I am still asking today.

From the Notebooks

Conversations preserved on paper.

Behind many of my published writings are years of notebooks, journals, handwritten manuscripts, typed pages, and unfinished ideas.

Some contain the beginnings of stories completed decades later. Others preserve questions and observations that remain interesting to me today.

Writing Across Time

One of the unexpected pleasures of revisiting earlier writings has been the opportunity to encounter the person who originally wrote them.

The younger writer did not know the experiences ahead of him. The older writer sometimes discovers that the younger one was asking questions he is still asking today.
